FAQ

Logo & Branding questions, answered.

How long does logo design take?

A logo usually takes 1 to 2 weeks from questionnaire to final files. A full brand identity takes 2 to 4 weeks.

What files will I receive?

You receive vector files (AI, SVG, PDF), PNG and JPG versions for web and social, plus a short brand guide covering colours, fonts and usage.

Do I own the logo?

Yes. Once the project is complete, you own the final logo and all rights to it.

Can you refresh my existing logo?

Yes. We can modernise your current logo while keeping the recognition you have already built.
