Questions

Good to know.

What services does Pixel and Brush offer?

We offer four services: custom web design, Google SEO and AEO (answer engine optimisation), social media content creation, and logo and branding. Most clients combine two or more so their brand looks and sounds the same everywhere.

Do you work with businesses outside Ottawa?

Yes. We are based in Ottawa, Ontario and work with clients across Canada and internationally. Everything runs smoothly over phone, WhatsApp and video calls.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. A full multi-page business website takes 3 to 5 weeks, depending on content and features.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps your website rank in Google search results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) structures your content so AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand it and recommend your business in their answers. We do both together.
