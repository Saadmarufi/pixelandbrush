FAQ

SEO & AEO questions, answered.

What is AEO?

AEO stands for answer engine optimisation. It is the practice of structuring your website content so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can understand it and recommend your business in their answers.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most businesses see early movement within 2 to 3 months and stronger results between 4 and 12 months, depending on competition and the state of the website.

Can you guarantee the number one spot on Google?

No honest agency can guarantee a specific ranking because Google controls its results. What we guarantee is proven, white-hat work and transparent monthly reporting on your progress.

Do you do local SEO for Google Maps?

Yes. We optimise your Google Business Profile and local signals so you show up in the map pack for searches in Ottawa and nearby areas.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not always. We start with an audit. If your current site has a solid foundation we optimise it; if it is holding you back we will explain why and what it would take.
