FAQ

Web Design questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. A landing page costs less than a full e-commerce store. Call (613) 519-3706 or WhatsApp us and we will send a clear, fixed quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. A full business website takes 3 to 5 weeks.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site we build is mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can refresh your current site or rebuild it completely while keeping your existing Google rankings safe with proper redirects.

Is SEO included with web design?

Every website includes on-page SEO basics: fast loading, meta tags, headings, schema and a sitemap. Ongoing SEO and AEO campaigns are available as a monthly service.
